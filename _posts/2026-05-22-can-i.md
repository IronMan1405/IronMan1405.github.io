title: "Can I?"
date: 2026-05-22

Can I be the poem <br>
And not the poet? <br>
The admired and not the admirer? <br>
The captured and not the capturer? <br>
The written and not the writer? <br>
The loved and not the lover?

Can I? <br>
For once in life <br>
Be the wish and not the wisher, <br>
Be the yearn and not the yearner.

I've lived as <br>
The poet, the writer, <br>
The admirer, the lover, <br>
The wisher, the yearner.

But for once... <br>
Can I be the moon, <br>
And not the moonstruck?

<div style="margin-left: 50%">
— Asahi Hoshizora
</div>
