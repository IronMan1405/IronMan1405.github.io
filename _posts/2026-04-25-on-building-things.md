I used to think building projects was about finishing them. <br>
Making things work. Moving on.

That's not it

Every project is just a way to expose what I don’t understand yet. <br>
And most of the time, the “bug” isn’t in the code, it’s in the assumptions I didn’t realize I made.

The interesting part isn’t when something works. <br>
It’s the moment I realize why it *shouldn’t have worked at all* based on how I thought it worked.

Building isn’t proof of knowledge. <br>
It’s a way to surface ignorance fast enough that you can actually do something about it.

I don’t build things to show what I know. <br>
I build things to find what I missed.
