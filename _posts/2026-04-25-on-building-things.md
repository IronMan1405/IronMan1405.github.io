I used to think building projects was about completing them. \
Finishing code, wiring circuits, making things “work.”

Now I think it’s something else.

Every project is just a way to expose what I don’t understand yet. \
A bug isn’t an error in the system, it’s a gap in my thinking.

The interesting part isn’t when something works. \
It’s the moment I realize why it shouldn’t have worked in the first place.

I don’t build things to show what I know. \
I build things to find what I missed.
