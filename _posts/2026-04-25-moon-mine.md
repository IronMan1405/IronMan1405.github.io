I want to make the moon mine

I want the moon to be mine, <br>
Due to which I might take away its shine, <br>
But the moon is still the moon, <br>
Whether it is a new, crescent, half, or full, the moon is still the moon.

I want the moon to be mine, <br>
On its good days, I desire the moon, <br>
I desire it the most, on its bad ones. <br>
I want the moon to be mine, <br>
Even if it loses its shine, <br>
I'll make it mine.

I wish for it to be mine, <br>
When it is full, <br>
More when it is halfway there and almost whole, <br>
Even more when it is crescent, <br>
The most when it is a new moon.

I want the moon to be mine <br>
In all its phases, which people named, <br>
To me, the moon is the same, <br>
In every phase people named.

At last, the moon shall be mine, <br>
May I be called selfish for making it mine, <br>
But 'tis what I desire, so it shall be mine.

Mine is what I want to call the moon, <br>
More than call it the moon. <br>

<div style="margin-left: 50%">
— Asahi Hoshizora
</div>
