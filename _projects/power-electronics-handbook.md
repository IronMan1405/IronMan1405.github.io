A practical handbook covering the fundamentals of power design for embedded systems and robotics projects.

Topics include capacitors, regulators, MOSFETs, grounding, power distribution, noise mitigation, battery systems, PCB layout, thermal design, and reliability engineering.

## Topics Covered

- Capacitors & Inductors
- Voltage Regulators
- Diodes & MOSFETs
- Grounding & Power Distribution
- Noise, EMI & Filtering
- Battery & Protection Design
- PCB Power Layout
- Reliability & Thermal Considerations
- System-Level Design Checklist
